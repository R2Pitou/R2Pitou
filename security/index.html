Overview

As a systems administrator and security lead, my work focuses on translating risk management principles into operational controls. Rather than treating security as an isolated compliance activity, I integrate protection policies directly into everyday workflows. This includes constructing access control parameters, investigating security incidents, conducting root cause analyses, and proposing standardized offboarding controls.

My hands-on experience covers directory deprovisioning audits, share access analysis, network segmentation, security awareness development, and the safe, risk-managed enablement of generative AI integrations.

Security Governance

Effective security governance is built on the systematic enforcement of access structures and organizational habits. My approach transitions administrative operations from a reactive posture to structured governance:

  • Least-privilege access management: Eliminating generic administrator profiles and enforcing role-specific permission delegation. Transitioning directories from broad "Editor" access to restricted "Viewer/Commenter" profiles by default (Performed).
  • Employee Lifecycle Alignment: Analyzing departure lag, highlighting offboarding gaps, and recommending automated HR-to-IT deprovisioning integrations to eliminate shadow accounts (Recommended; not implemented during tenure).
  • Log Auditing and Alerting: Standardizing directory monitoring logs. Configuring explicit alert thresholds to detect anomalies, such as bulk document downloads or external OAuth shares, before they escalate.

AI Governance & Enablement

Rather than enforcing outright prohibitions that users will bypass through personal devices (shadow IT), my approach establishes clear guardrails that enable safe productivity. When integrating generative AI tools, I structure governance around:

  • Responsible AI Adoption: Defining which datasets are permitted for LLM exposure and maintaining clear boundaries to protect proprietary information.
  • Practical Guardrails: Restricting standard API keys from accessing production environments without administrative sandbox review. Integrating content filtering to block malicious or unvetted scripts.
  • AI Literacy & Enablement: Constructing Acceptable Use Policies and operational charters that educate staff on prompt engineering safety, checks for accuracy, and regulatory guidelines.

Security Philosophy

"Security exists to enable organizations to operate safely and confidently, not to block useful work."

Absolutist security models that completely restrict user operations fail because they incentivize personnel to design unauthorized workarounds. A practical security model recognizes that the primary objective is risk reduction balanced with operational efficiency. My goal is to deliver secure, standardized, and fully documented directory operations that protect organizational continuity without creating friction for personnel.
