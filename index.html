What this site is for

This site is the public front door for my Google Workspace security review work.

The focus is practical and leadership-readable:

  • risk visibility
  • offboarding and identity control
  • alert routing and auditability
  • shared drive governance
  • remediation steps ranked by impact versus effort

I do not treat technical issues as isolated accidents. I look for the structural weaknesses underneath them.
