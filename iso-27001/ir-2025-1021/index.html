Incident Report

IR-2025-1021

Unauthorized Information Modification and Deletion

This incident was classified as a major non-conformity affecting integrity and availability. It involved unauthorized modification, deletion, and restructuring events across core institutional shared drives.

Executive Summary

What happened

On October 21, 2025, an identified actor initiated unauthorized modification and deletion events across core institutional shared drives. The incident resulted in destruction of academic records and unauthorized restructuring of the information hierarchy.

Technical Pattern

Observed pattern

The report describes:

  • mass trash events targeting Teacher Drive and Pre Testing
  • unauthorized renaming and settings changes affecting core shared drives
  • creation of new folder hierarchies and movement of data outside standard administrative workflows

Root Cause

Why it was possible

The report identifies two main failures:

  • excessive privileged access that exceeded operational requirements
  • logging without alerting strong enough to halt mass deletion in real time

Corrective Direction

Corrective action

The report recommends immediate containment through suspension and blocking, plus long-term reduction of permissions by moving non-owner staff to default view/comment access unless elevated rights are justified.
