Incident Report

IR-2026-0108

Post-Employment Data Exfiltration

This incident was classified as a critical breach of confidentiality. It involved a massive exfiltration event of 20,000+ files by a former employee after their official termination date.

Executive Summary

What happened

The report states that credentials remained active after the subject's official termination date, permitting sustained access from multiple IP addresses and enabling a high-volume download event involving institutional materials and staff PII.

Why It Mattered

Why this was serious

The exfiltrated material included pacing guides, teacher resources, strategic institutional documents, exam-related material, and highly sensitive personal records. The report frames this as a total failure of offboarding.

Root Cause

Why it was possible

The report identifies:

  • failure to revoke access at end of employment
  • no formal shared HR-IT offboarding checklist
  • no data leakage prevention or anomaly-based download monitoring strong enough to catch the activity early

Corrective Direction

Corrective action

The report recommends:

  • a joint HR-IT offboarding policy
  • automated account suspension through HRIS integration
  • monitoring activities capable of flagging anomalous download behavior
