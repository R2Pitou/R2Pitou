Leadership Preview

Google Workspace Risk Exposure Baseline for Multi-Campus Schools

This scorecard is a leadership preview, not a full assessment of a named institution. Its purpose is to show the kind of risk framing, control review, and remediation guidance that can be delivered quickly for school leadership.

The sample positions three areas as urgent red-zone exposure:

  • offboarding and identity lifecycle
  • logging and human alert routing
  • shared drive governance

What It Is

What this scorecard is

This is a compact, traffic-light style review artifact designed for leadership skimming.

It is meant to answer three questions fast:

  • where exposure is likely to be concentrated
  • which controls are usually missing or drifting
  • what the first 30 days of remediation should achieve

Who It Is For

Who this is for

This review format is designed for:

  • multi-campus schools
  • Google Workspace environments with many staff accounts
  • organizations that suspect process drift but do not yet have a clean control picture
  • leadership teams that need a readable summary before committing to deeper technical work

Evidence

How the review is grounded

The sample states that evidence is gathered through:

  • Admin Console policy review
  • identity and offboarding control checks
  • Drive and external-sharing review
  • log retention and alert-routing review

Outputs

What leadership receives

The sample defines the output as:

  • a traffic-light risk summary
  • control gaps by risk area
  • mitigation steps ranked by impact versus effort

Engagement Shape

Typical review shape

The sample states a typical audit duration of 3 to 5 working days and leadership time required of about 90 minutes total.

First 30 Days

Typical first 30-day outcomes

The sample lists these likely early improvements:

  • former staff access boundary enforced automatically
  • alert routing assigned to named roles
  • shared drive ownership model restored
  • external sharing defaults tightened
  • legacy access paths removed
